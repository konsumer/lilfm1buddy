lilfm1buddy

Switch the M-VAVE FM-1's firmware from the FM-1 itself: a tiny ESP32-S3 on its USB port, a menu on its screen, no computer.

Install Firmwares Use it

You need

  • Seeed XIAO ESP32S3 Plus: 21 × 18 mm, room for about 20 firmwares, a battery charger on board, and it reads its battery (the FM-1 shows its level). Its LED blinks what it's doing.
  • a 3.7 V 180 mAh LiPo with bare wires: solder it to the BAT+ and BAT− pads on the XIAO's back. Check the polarity with a meter first: the wire colours on these aren't always right. A firmware switch takes a few mAh, and the XIAO charges it from its USB-C (its red LED flashes while it does, and goes out when it's full).
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer, and a USB-C cable that carries data

No power switch: when it's done, or nothing happens for a while, the board goes to deep sleep (months on that battery). Press its reset button to wake it.

1. The program

Plug the XIAO into this computer and press Install. A new board: let it erase. To update one, don't: its firmwares stay.

Your browser can't: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page has to be opened over https.

No build yet: the page has no firmware next to it.

2. JieLi's loader, and firmwares

The board writes the FM-1's flash with the loader JieLi made for its chip (wl82loader.bin). It isn't ours to hand out: this page gets it from jl-uboot-tool, checks it, and puts it on the board. Then add the firmwares you want on it.

Until a firmware is picked nothing is written: to keep what's on it, switch the FM-1 off and on. Only the firmware's own image is written, never the flash's head (what the chip starts from), and the chip's boot loader is in its ROM: nothing here can make it unrecoverable.
