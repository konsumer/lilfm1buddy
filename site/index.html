lilfm1buddy

Switch the M-VAVE FM-1's firmware from the FM-1 itself: a tiny ESP32-S3 on its USB port, a menu on its screen, no computer.

Install Firmwares Use it

You need

  • an ESP32-S3 board with 16 MB flash and PSRAM (N16R8, like the ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 N16R8), its USB-C port on the chip's own USB (not a USB-serial chip). Its RGB LED (GPIO 48) says what it's doing.
  • a battery for it (it's the FM-1's USB host: the FM-1 on its own battery, the two on one USB-C data cable)
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer, and a USB-C cable that carries data

1. The program

Plug the board into this computer and press Install. A new board: let it erase. To update one, don't: its firmwares stay.

Your browser can't: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page has to be opened over https.

No build yet: the page has no firmware next to it.

2. JieLi's loader, and firmwares

The board writes the FM-1's flash with the loader JieLi made for its chip (wl82loader.bin). It isn't ours to hand out: this page gets it from jl-uboot-tool, checks it, and puts it on the board. Then add the firmwares you want on it.

Until a firmware is picked nothing is written: switching the FM-1 off and on brings its firmware back. Only the firmware's own image is written, never the flash's head (what the chip starts from), and the chip's boot loader is in its ROM: nothing here can make it unrecoverable.
